# Sales-Dashboard
### 1. Project Title / Headline

**Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI**

 ### 2. Short Description / Purpose

An interactive **Sales Analysis Dashboard** developed in Power BI to analyze sales performance, identify business trends, and provide actionable insights into revenue, products, customers, and overall business performance.

### 3. Tech Stack

* **Power BI** – Dashboard development & visualization
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – Calculated measures and KPIs
* **Excel/CSV** – Data handling and analysis

### 4. Data Source

The dashboard was created using a **sales dataset** containing transactional/business sales information. The data was cleaned, transformed, and prepared using Power Query before being analyzed and visualized in Power BI.

### 5. Features / Highlights

## Business Problem

Businesses often generate large amounts of sales data, but raw data can be difficult to interpret and use for decision-making. The key challenge is to understand sales performance, identify trends, recognize high-performing products and categories, and determine areas that require improvement.

## Goal of the Dashboard

The goal of this Power BI dashboard is to transform raw sales data into an **interactive and easy-to-understand business intelligence dashboard**. It helps users monitor key sales KPIs, analyze performance, identify trends, and make data-driven business decisions.

## Walkthrough of Key Visuals

* **KPI Cards:** Display important metrics such as Total Sales, Profit, Orders, and Quantity.
* **Sales Trend Chart:** Shows changes in sales performance over time and helps identify growth or declining trends.
* **Product/Category Analysis:** Highlights top-performing and underperforming products or categories.
* **Customer Analysis:** Provides insights into customer purchasing behavior and sales contribution.
* **Interactive Filters & Slicers:** Allow users to filter the dashboard by relevant dimensions such as date, product, category, or customer.
* **Performance Charts:** Compare sales and profitability across different segments to identify business opportunities.

## Business Impact & Insights

* Provides management with a **centralized view of sales performance**.
* Helps identify **top-performing products and categories**.
* Makes it easier to monitor **sales and profit trends**.
* Supports identification of **underperforming areas** that may require strategic attention.
* Reduces manual reporting effort by providing an **interactive and automated dashboard**.
* Enables faster and more informed **data-driven decision-making**.
